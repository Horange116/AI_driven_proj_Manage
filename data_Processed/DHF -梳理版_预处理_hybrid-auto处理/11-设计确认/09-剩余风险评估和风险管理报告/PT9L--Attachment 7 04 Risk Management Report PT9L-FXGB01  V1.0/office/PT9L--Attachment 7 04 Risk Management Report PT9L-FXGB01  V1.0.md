# 1. **Overview**

<a id="_Toc30149"></a>
### **1.1 Introduction of the product**

<a id="_Toc3055"></a>
This product is a high-tech infra-red (IR) thermometer designed to take human body temperature by measuring the energy of IR emitted from the forehead. The product will help you to assess you and your family members’ health conditions easily and quickly.

<a id="_Toc31475"></a>
### **1.2 Introduction on implementation of the risk management plan**

<a id="_Toc4045"></a>
The risk management activity including the risk management plan was began at the time the project is approved.

The risk management plan has defined the acceptance criteria of risk, and has also arranged the risk management review activity. The risk management group is established to ensure the risk management activity of the current project will be implemented effectively according to the risk management plan.

On the development phase and prototype phase of the Infrared Forehead Thermometer, the risk management group reviewed the risk management respectively, and documented the risk management files.

The objective of this review is to take a complete evaluation on risk management activity performed on each phase before the product is marketed, to ensure that the risk management plan has been accomplished.  And according to evaluating the risk analysis, risk assessment and control, and the acceptance of the residual risk, to ensure that all the risks has been managed and all the residual risk can be accepted.

# 7. **Conclusion of Risk Management Review** 

Overall risk-benefit analysis:

The Intended use of PT9L is: The Infrared Forehead Thermometer is intended for the intermittent measurement of body temperature from the forehead skin surface on people of all ages. It can be used by consumers in the household environment and by healthcare providers.

From the clinical evaluation report ,we can conclude :

clinical safety outcomes can prove that the product is safe; clinical performance outcomes can prove that  PT9L can achieve its intended use. Users can benefit from proper use, benefits outweigh the overall residual risks,so the overall residual risks are acceptable.

Risk Management Group has reviewed the PT9L, and concluded the following:

——Risk management plan has been performed properly:

The risk management process has been implemented according to the criteria established in the risk 	management plan standards,the risk team have considered the efficiency and safety of the 	product,and think that all the identified risks have been controlled as far as possible.

——Residual risks are acceptable:

All single residual risks are in the “Acceptable” area. And the distribution of all the residual risks less 	than 1/2 of the overall residual risks are in the higher severity regions or in the higher probability 	regions. And the clusters of all the residual risks are not borderline.Compared with the intended benefits for PT9L ,the benefits outweigh the overall residual risks, So the overall residual risks are 	also acceptable.

——The method of getting information of production and after production is proper.

In Conclusion, the medical benefits of the intended use outweigh the overall residual risk.
